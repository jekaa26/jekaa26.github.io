Private • Personal use

Four AI assistants. One personal workspace.

Hermes Personal Assistants is a self-hosted, private system of Telegram-based AI helpers for organizing everyday life. It is designed for use by its owner, not offered as a public service.

Google access: read-only

🧭 Planner

Helps organize schedules and to-dos. With permission, it reads events from Google Calendar and lists or tasks from Google Tasks.

🌿 Trips

Helps research travel and plan itineraries independently of Planner's Google connection.

💬 Reflection & 💡 Ideas

Separate assistants for conversations, reflection, brainstorming and creative ideas.

Your Google data remains under your control. Google access is limited to the Planner profile and requires explicit consent. The integration requests read-only Calendar and Tasks permissions and cannot create, edit or delete your Google events or tasks.

How Google data is used

Planner retrieves Calendar events and Google Tasks information only to help with personal planning and answer authorized requests. The system is self-hosted on a private server; AI model services may process information necessary to generate responses. See the privacy policy for details on access, processing, storage and revocation.
