Purpose

Hermes Personal Assistants is a private, experimental application for the owner's personal productivity, travel planning, reflection and brainstorming. It is not offered for public registration or commercial use.

Google integration

Only the Planner assistant currently uses Google OAuth to read authorized Google Calendar and Google Tasks data. Permission is voluntary and can be revoked through Google Account settings. The integration does not write or delete events or tasks.

AI-generated results

AI assistant responses can be incomplete or inaccurate. Important decisions and scheduling details should be checked against original sources.
