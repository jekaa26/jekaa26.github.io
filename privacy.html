Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. About this application

Hermes Personal Assistants is a private, self-hosted set of Telegram-based AI helpers intended for the owner's personal use. The Google integration is enabled only for the Planner assistant and connects to Google services with the account owner's consent. It is not a general-purpose public application.

2. Information accessed

With explicit Google OAuth authorization, Planner can access:

  • Google Calendar (read-only): calendar names, metadata, events and schedule details needed to answer planning questions.
  • Google Tasks (read-only): task-list names, tasks and their details needed for personal planning.
  • Account identification: Google account email and basic identity details as needed to associate authorization with the correct account.

Planner does not request permission to create, modify or delete Google Calendar events or Google Tasks.

3. Use of information

Information retrieved from Google is used to respond to the authorized user's questions, summarize schedules and tasks, and provide personal planning assistance. Google data is not sold, used to serve advertisements, or shared with other end users.

4. Processing and service providers

The application runs on a privately managed server and communicates with Google APIs to retrieve authorized data. Relevant calendar or task data may be passed to the configured external AI model provider to generate the user's requested answer. Replies may be delivered via Telegram. Each external service processes submitted content under its applicable terms and policies. Other assistants are not configured with access to the Planner Google integration.

5. Storage, security and retention

OAuth credentials are saved in private server-side storage to maintain the integration and refresh authorization. Account passwords are not requested or stored by the application. Retrieved information may appear in private conversation histories, system logs or assistant memories, depending on server configuration. Access to these records and credentials is controlled by the server owner. Stored information can be deleted by the owner. No public Google-data directory is provided.

6. Control and revocation

You can revoke this application's Google access at any time through Google Account – Third-party connections. Revocation stops new authorized retrievals but does not by itself delete information already retained in local logs or conversation history. Requests concerning locally stored data may be sent to the contact below.

7. Changes

Material changes to Google scopes or data practices will be reflected here. The date above identifies the latest revision.
